### INFO
My dotfiles I use for my Arch Linux desktop. I'm using i3wm + alacritty on Xorg/X11. Make sure you're pasting these files in the right places and not just blind copying them, it takes a slight amount of implimentation.  

My wallpaper is one that I made and as such I mastered it for my 1280x1024 device screen. I may make a full 16:9 adapt at some point but for now you will most likely have to replace it with your own wallpaper.  

### FONTS
Make sure you have `ttc-iosevka-ss08` as well as the font `dragon_alphabet` (located here in /fonts (place it in ~/.local/share/fonts)).  
 
I also made Pelagiad available to you but not even **I** use it as it's bad for heavy terminal use. Both the Dragon Alphabet and Pelagiad fonts come from the OpenMW project.
